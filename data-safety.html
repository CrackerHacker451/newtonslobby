3. Zero-Knowledge Proofs & Biometrics Exemption

Newton's Lobby integrates World ID 4.0 IDKit for sybil resistance and student verification. We do not collect, process, or store biometric data (e.g. iris scans or facial geometry) on our servers or within the app.

  • Biometric verification is handled entirely within the World App on the user's hardware device.
  • Our servers only receive a one-way cryptographic SHA-256 nullifier hash verifying uniqueness without revealing the user's identity.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Protocol

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users have the right to request deletion of their account and all personal information:

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

  • In-App: Open Newton's Lobby > Navigate to Profile / Settings > Tap Delete Account & Data.
  • Online Web Portal: Submit directly via our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Request Page.
  • Via Email: Send an email from your registered university address to dtgtgm@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - [Your Username].

Upon verification, all account records, user profile information, messages, and uploaded challenge proofs will be permanently deleted from our live production databases within 30 days.
