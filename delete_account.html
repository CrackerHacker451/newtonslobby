Official Data Deletion & Privacy Policy

Newton's Lobby — Account & Data Deletion Request

Newton's Lobby (package name: com.Newtons_Lobby.myapp) respects user privacy rights and provides full control over user data deletion in compliance with Google Play Policy.

1. How to Delete Your Account Inside the App

You can delete your account and all associated personal data immediately inside the Newton's Lobby Android Application:

  1. Open the Newton's Lobby App on your Android phone.
  2. Go to the Profile Settings ⚙️ tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account & Data 🗑️.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your user profile, messages, posts, and wallet data will be permanently deleted immediately.

2. Online Web Account Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the mobile application and wish to request the deletion of your account and personal data, please email our data protection officer or use the direct mail action below:

Data Safety Contact Email: dtgtgm@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request — [Your Email / User ID]

Processing Time: All web account deletion requests are completed within 48 hours.

✉️ Send Account Deletion Request Email

3. What Data Is Permanently Purged

  • Firebase Authentication User Profile & Account ID
  • Firestore Student Record & UniCoins Wallet Balance
  • In-App Direct & Group Chat Message History
  • DareDrop Challenge Submissions, Photos, Videos & Comments
  • Device Tokens (FCM tokens for push notifications)

4. Data Retention Policy

Upon account deletion, all personal data stored in our database is permanently erased. No user data is retained or sold to third parties.
