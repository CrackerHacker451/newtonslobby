Newton's Lobby — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026. Official Website: https://www.newtonslobby.com. Newton's Lobby (package name: com.Newtons_Lobby.myapp) is committed to protecting user privacy and data security.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, university email address, and profile photo provided during account creation or Google Sign-In.
  • User Content: Study notes, DareDrop video and photo challenge proofs, and 1-on-1/group chat messages sent within the app.
  • Device & Usage Data: Firebase App Check tokens, notification tokens for FCM push alerts, and approximate location data when enabled for campus radar discovery.

2. World ID Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Newton's Lobby integrates World ID 4.0 IDKit and World ID Agent Pass (https://auth.world.org/). We use zero-knowledge proofs and SHA-256 nullifier hashes to verify unique human status without storing any biometric data or real-world identity files on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide campus networking, real-time 1080p video calls, study note sharing, and zero-knowledge AI proof verification. We never sell or lease user data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is secured using Firebase Cloud Storage, Firestore persistent encryption, and Arkiv Network DB-Chains. Heavy files in the Study Vault are encrypted using AES-256 and stored on Swarm decentralized storage.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You can request account deletion at any time directly inside the app under Profile Settings, visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Request Page, or email our designated privacy officer at dtgtgm@gmail.com. Upon account deletion, all associated user records are permanently purged within 48 hours.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or data rights requests, contact us at: dtgtgm@gmail.com
